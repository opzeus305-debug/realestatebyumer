The spire first. Then the shafts. Then one flash of light.

The mark assembles the way the tower was built: the needle goes up first and the massing rises to meet it. 02 · Page transition

A curtain that climbs as the tower’s own silhouette.

Between routes a night curtain rises from the bottom of the viewport. Its top edge is not flat: it is the mark’s setback profile — five columns climbing at different lags, the spire strip racing ahead — so for half a second the whole screen is the logo. At full cover the destination’s elevation marker appears; then the curtain continues upward and off. Back and forward navigation skip it.

03 · Loading and progress

The spire is the progress bar.

The letters are always lit; the spire is a dim track that fills from its base. The same number drives the large mark, the 40px nav mark and the 1px margin line with its ten level notches — so the preloader, the nav and the page all read the same instrument. No percentage counter in production; the figure here is for reviewers. At 100% the tip glints once.

04 · Idle and the earned sweep

At 24px it does nothing. That is the design.

The nav mark is a silhouette: the hairline letterforms are thickened uniformly with a non-scaling stroke so the massing and spire hold at 24–40px, flat champagne, no gradient below 120px. It never rotates, pulses or breathes. Hairlines around it warm on hover. The specular sweep is an event, not an idle: it fires once when the visitor first reaches the Spire levels, once on a successful form, and never on a loop.

  • Idlezero motion · flat champagne-300 · stroke 1.1px non-scaling
  • Hoveradjacent hairline → hairline-gold · 180ms ease-silk
  • Sweep900ms · ease-in-out-soft · skewed band · ≤ 1 per session
  • Reducednever fires

05 · The handoff

The letters become the tower.

The four most valuable seconds on the site. The wordmark, still flat and gold, gains the depth of glass as the camera begins to move. Then the crystal rises from the floor inside the letters’ own footprints — each shaft exactly as tall and as wide as the letter it replaces, the M carrying the spire — while the letterforms dissolve upward into champagne dust that gathers into the glass. What remains is the Spire Prism, the hero object, already turning. The logo did not cut to the crystal. It grew into it.

  • 1.0 → 2.8sshafts rise from y=0 · M → E → U → R · 1400ms out-expo · stagger 90ms
  • 1.2 → 3.0sletters dissolve · 2,600 points rise and gather to the shaft axes
  • 2.8s →prism holds · 0.05 rad/s · one light sweep across the facets
